# NuraSpecs Nigeria Paid Media Launch Plan

Primary market: Nigeria
Primary conversion: Successful demo enquiry submitted through nuraspecs.com

## Campaign architecture

### 1. NuraSpecs One
Audience:
- Founders and business owners
- COOs / operations leaders
- Procurement and finance managers
- Administrative and transformation leaders
- Technology decision-makers in growing organisations

Core pain:
Disconnected approvals, spreadsheets, email/WhatsApp follow-up, limited operational visibility.

Core message:
Bring requests, approvals, procurement visibility and decision tracking into one connected workspace.

Landing page:
https://nuraspecs.com/campaign-one-ng.html

### 2. School Management
Audience:
- Private school proprietors
- Principals and head teachers
- School administrators
- Bursars / finance administrators
- Education consultants and school operators

Core pain:
Admissions, attendance, fees and records managed across disconnected spreadsheets and tools.

Core message:
Run admissions, students, attendance, fees, payments and reporting from one clearer administrative platform.

Landing page:
https://nuraspecs.com/campaign-school-ng.html

## Initial 14-day test budget

Total: NGN 420,000

- Meta / Facebook / Instagram: NGN 210,000 (NGN 15,000/day)
- YouTube / Google Demand Gen: NGN 126,000 (NGN 9,000/day)
- X: NGN 84,000 (NGN 6,000/day)

Split each platform approximately 60% NuraSpecs One / 40% School Management for the first test.
After 7 days, reallocate toward the product/platform combination generating the strongest qualified demo enquiries.

## Conversion event

Primary event:
demo_enquiry_success

Fire only after /api/contact returns a successful response.

Recommended platform integrations:
- Meta Pixel / Conversions API Lead event
- Google Ads website conversion for completed demo enquiry
- X Pixel website conversion event

Do not optimize campaigns only for page visits once conversion tracking is available.

## First-week evaluation

Watch:
- Qualified demo enquiries
- Cost per qualified enquiry
- Landing-page conversion rate
- Click-through rate
- Cost per landing-page view
- Video 25% / 50% / 75% completion rates
- Product split: NuraSpecs One vs School Management
- Geography breakdown within Nigeria

Do not move budget based only on cheapest clicks. Prefer campaigns generating relevant organisations and actual demo enquiries.
