Website privacy information

This page describes the functionality of our current public website. Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Interactive demonstrations

The website's interactive product tours operate within your browser using fictional sample information. They are separate from live customer environments for both School Management and NuraSpecs One. Do not enter real customer, staff, student or financial details into a demonstration.

Contact and demonstration enquiries

The enquiry form sends the details you provide to the NuraSpecs team through our Cloudflare-hosted contact endpoint so we can respond to your request. The public website does not use a customer-facing account database for these enquiries. If you arrive through a campaign link, standard campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium or utm_campaign may be kept for the duration of your browser session and included with your enquiry so we can understand which campaign generated the request. WhatsApp or your email provider may independently process communications under their own terms.

Hosting and third-party services

Our public website is intended for static hosting through Cloudflare. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the service. Our published website build does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. The current public build does not include third-party advertising pixels or analytics scripts. If these are introduced later, this notice should be updated before activation.

Your questions

For questions about information you have shared with us, requests relating to your personal information, or details of our current handling arrangements, contact info@nuraspecs.com. Rights and obligations depend on the laws applicable to your situation.

This page addresses the NuraSpecs corporate website; individual products may have separate customer privacy notices and contractual terms.
