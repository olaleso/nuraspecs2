Privacy Policy

Starter website privacy notice for Nuraspecs Solutions.

Before production launch: this is a starter website notice, not final legal advice. Review it against the actual analytics, cookies, enquiry processing and data-handling practices used on the deployed website.

1. Information you provide

If you contact Nuraspecs Solutions by email, WhatsApp or through the website enquiry form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation or school and the contents of your enquiry.

2. How information may be used

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange demonstrations, discuss trials and subscriptions, understand project requirements and maintain relevant business correspondence.

3. Website forms

The current GitHub Pages version does not store form submissions on a Nuraspecs server. The contact form opens the visitor's email application with the enquiry pre-filled. This behaviour should be updated in this policy if a form-processing service or API is introduced.

4. Analytics and cookies

No analytics platform is included in this starter build. If analytics, marketing tags or non-essential cookies are added, the privacy and cookie information should be updated before deployment.

5. Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent to info@nuraspecs.com.
